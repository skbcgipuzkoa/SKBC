# Deferred students and a simpler planning screen

User requests that students who choose the next call remain in exam planning for that next configured call, and that Próximos exámenes become substantially simpler.

- Keep the original declined/deferred invitation and response unchanged. Derive a planning floor from the latest valid same-grade/class/cycle deferral; choose a configured call in a strictly later month. Honour that floor for explicit actual-day checks too. Do not automatically invite, approve, register, pass or promote anyone.
- Re-evaluate feasibility for the later call. Hard impossibility still excludes candidates; a deferred student who can mathematically prepare is shown for teacher review. Explain the prior deferral and link to the later month; missing future configuration remains explicit rather than inventing a date.
- A deferred participant must not block confirmation of the examination date for the remaining participants. Preserve all prior responses, messages and reservations. A later live invitation takes precedence over an older deferred response in the own ficha.
- Replace repeated banners, metrics, colour blocks and full row explanations with one month/search/group control and three clear tabs: Por convocar, Convocados y respuestas, Seguimiento. Show short selectable student rows/cards and expandable preparation. Keep technical rules, recalculation, stored semaphores and unusual views in advanced details. Colours remain scoped to the whole selected call, independent of pending invitations.
- Called counts and response lists use invitation month, even when a deferred student's forecast moves to a later month. Current-month response history remains visible. Pending lists exclude already-called students for that month and hard-impossible candidates; selection and backend duplicate guards remain.
- Regression tests cover deferral boundaries, different grades/cycles, missing calls, exact-date overrides, response preservation, all-called/empty states, month/search/group scopes and unchanged four student criteria. Independent review, build and production publication; no real approvals or notifications during tests.
